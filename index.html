The full pipeline

From Raw Model to Shipped Asset

Two add-ons built to work as one continuous workflow — not two separate tools you happen to own.

01

Build the surface

MatricX PRO: apply a material, stack Edge Wear, Dirt or Rust, paint where wear goes, add decals and trims.

02

Bake the textures

MatricX Bake: bake every layer and decal into one clean PBR texture set — per object or one shared atlas.

03

Ship it

MatricX Bake: export FBX or GLB with its textures, ready for Unity, Unreal Engine or Godot.

FAQ

Before You Buy

Do I need both add-ons, or can I use Bake without PRO?

MatricX Bake works with any Blender material, not just MatricX PRO's. The bundle exists for the combined workflow, but each add-on is fully independent if that's all you need.

What's the difference between the four tiers?

All four include the complete MatricX PRO and MatricX Bake. Higher tiers add priority support, multi-seat licensing and direct collaboration — not extra core features.

What does the multi-seat license cover?

Studio and Enterprise include a multi-seat license for up to 10 seats, for small studios and teams working across both add-ons.

Is the launch price permanent?

No. The launch offer takes 38% off every tier for a limited time. Regular prices are $109, $318, $786 and $2,485.

Which Blender versions are supported?

Blender 4.5 and newer for both add-ons, tested on 4.5, 5.1 and 5.2. Windows, macOS and Linux.

Where do I find the documentation?

Read the MatricX PRO guide and the MatricX Bake guide. The Bundle guide shows how they work together.
