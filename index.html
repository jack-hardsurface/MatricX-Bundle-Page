03 / Why Together

Built to work as
one pipeline, not two tools.

This isn't just a discount on two separate add-ons — MatricX Bake has real, specific compensation built for MatricX Pro's materials.

MatricX Pro's imperfection layers frequently use Object or Generated coordinates inside nested node groups. Joining objects normally breaks those masks. MatricX Bake stores each source object's original coordinates and substitutes them only on temporary copies, so dirt, wear, noise and bump masks keep their original placement when baked — with channel-consistent compensation so Dirt and Edge Wear bake at the strength they were authored at, not stronger or weaker.

In short: the surface you build in Pro is the surface that ships, not an approximation of it.

05 / Before You Buy

The things
you should know.

Do I need both add-ons, or can I use Bake without Pro?

MatricX Bake works with any Blender material, not just MatricX Pro's. The bundle exists for the combined workflow, but Bake is fully independent if that's all you need.

What's the difference between the four bundle tiers?

All four include the complete MatricX Pro and MatricX Bake feature set. Higher tiers add priority support, multi-seat licensing, and direct collaboration — not extra core features.

What does the multi-seat license cover?

Studio Bundle and above include a multi-seat license for up to 10 seats, intended for small studios and teams working across both add-ons.

Which Blender versions are supported?

Blender 4.5 and newer for both add-ons — MatricX Pro is tested through Blender 5.2, and MatricX Bake through Blender 5.2 as well.

Where do I find documentation for each add-on?

Read the MatricX Pro guide or the MatricX Bake guide.
